# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. Both come from the Steam library hero image of Survival Log (1920x620, the key art with no logo), the same source as `assets/gameart.jpg`. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images.

- `thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080) in the style of the mod thumbnails: on black, "Survival" and "Log" in the gold `(251, 178, 11)` (Noto Sans Bold), right-aligned on the left, "Vortex Extension" in white (Noto Sans Light) under them, and a 960x620 cut of the key art around the character on the right, with rounded corners. The gallery image. Pick it as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `banner.jpg` - the mod page background (1300x372): a 1440x412 cut of the key art around the character, scaled down, with no text.
